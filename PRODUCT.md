## Platform

web

## Users

Hiring teams evaluating Vatsal Jain for applied AI, solutions, forward-deployed, and enablement roles are the primary audience, confirmed October 27, 2026. Potential collaborators and Clarity Works clients are secondary.

## Product Purpose

Help a visitor quickly understand who Vatsal is, see credible examples of his work, and start a conversation. The October 28 redesign responds to feedback that the previous multipage release kept the old visual design. The site should feel personal, visually engaging, and easy to scan on each page.

## Positioning

A founder and practical AI builder who combines workflow discovery, system design, and capability transfer. Personal contribution and project maturity stay explicit.

## Operating Context

Visitors may scan on a phone before opening a selected case study. The homepage introduces the person and work; detailed architecture, claims, and caveats remain available on dedicated routes.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript generated by Python. Home plus dedicated Work, About, Notes & videos, and Contact pages; six project pages, two notes, external video links, email, and the verified Cal.com introductory-call link. Preserve every existing content route. Deploy only dist/ through the existing GitHub/Vercel integration. Keep production-only analytics, self-hosted fonts, hashed responsive assets, and canonical/domain routing. Performance target: at least 95; accessibility, best practices, and SEO at least 99.

## Brand Commitments

Vatsal Jain; founder of Clarity Works. Preserve the user-selected palette #BFBDC1, #6D6A75, #37323E, #DEB841, #DE9E36 and dark text on gold actions. The user supplied Katie Ko, Yoran Beisher, Sid Haldar, and Althra as references for a more appealing personal-brand site. No emoji arrows. The user confirmed a code-first redesign using references and real project assets.

## Evidence on Hand

Public copy in content.json; provenance in the private workspace's profile/evidence.md. Portrait and outdoor photo are approved personal assets; two video thumbnails are from Vatsal's public videos. Six supported project stories include a live client pilot, outbound engine, Infnty under development, LeadPulse, content production, and capability advisory. Infnty has 20 user-reported early testers onboarded, not verified active usage. Outbound throughput is user-reported 300 messages/day (November 6, 2026); 1,000+/day is design capacity only. Do not publish private records, screenshots with participant data, invented interfaces, clients, testimonials, or results. Any explanatory mockup must be clearly illustrative.

## Product Principles

- Show the person and concrete work before explaining the process.
- Make the homepage useful in a quick scan; offer depth on demand.
- Keep claims attached to their actual scope and project stage.
- Make contacting Vatsal simple.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable phone layouts, semantic controls, visible keyboard focus, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support, and content visible when JavaScript is unavailable.
